# Code of conduct

ReliaForge contributors are expected to communicate with respect, assume good faith, and make
technical decisions based on evidence. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens
the safety or effectiveness of the community. Report conduct concerns through a private channel
provided by the repository host. Reports will be reviewed discreetly and without retaliation.
